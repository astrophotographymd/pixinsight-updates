# License delivery email template

Use this after a paid order for AstroMD Social Export. Fill the placeholders, attach nothing that contains other customers' keys, and send from astrophotographymd@gmail.com only when Jim has approved sending that specific message.

Do not put a live license key in a website page, a chat, or a public post. Paste the key only in the email to that buyer.

Subject: Your AstroMD Social Export license key

Hi {{first_name}},

Thank you for buying AstroMD Social Export. Here is your license key.

Email to enter in the script: {{order_email}}
License key: {{license_key}}

You can use this key on up to two computers, on the honor system.

Install and unlock:

1. In PixInsight, go to Resources > Updates > Manage Repositories and add https://updates.astrophotographymd.com/
2. Run Resources > Updates > Check for Updates, install, and restart PixInsight if you are asked to.
3. Open a stretched image, then run Script > Astrophotography MD > Social Export.
4. Click Enter license key, type the email and key above, and click Unlock. The banner should read "Licensed to" followed by your email.

A short guide is here:
https://www.astrophotographymd.com/pages/social-export-guide

If Check for Updates does not find the script, or Unlock is rejected, reply to this email with your order number ({{order_number}}) and we will help.

Thank you,
Jim
