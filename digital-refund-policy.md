Astrophotography MD LLC ("AstroMD," "we," or "us") sells digital goods, including the AstroMD Social Export license. This policy explains when we will refund a digital purchase. Print orders and other physical products, if any, follow their own return terms.

## When we will refund a license

If you buy an AstroMD Social Export license and, within 14 days of the purchase, the script will not install or will not run in a supported copy of PixInsight (version 1.8.9 or later), email astrophotographymd@gmail.com. Please include your order number, PixInsight version, and operating system. We will help you get it working. If we cannot, we will refund the purchase price and revoke the license key.

We will also refund the purchase if you never received a license key and we cannot deliver one after you write to us.

## When we may decline a refund

We may decline a refund if:

- more than 14 days have passed since the purchase, and the script installed and ran as described;
- the request is only that you no longer want the product after a successful unlock and use;
- the problem comes from a computer, PixInsight install, or OS configuration that we cannot reasonably support, after we have tried to help;
- the license key was shared or published in a way that breaks the EULA.

If a platform (for example Instagram) changes its file-size or dimension rules after you buy the script, that is not, by itself, a reason for a refund. We aim to keep the script current through the update repository.

## How a refund is paid

Refunds are returned to the original payment method when the processor allows it. Once a refund is issued, the license key is no longer valid, and paid features return to the locked state.

## Ohio sales tax

If tax was collected on the purchase, a refund of the purchase will include that tax, and we will report it on the matching Ohio return.
